    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). Free roam stays open after the story, but there is no mission replay — so do the Chapter 1 meditation before Chapter 1 ends. Work the six stages in order; each stage lists the trophies it earns.

      Missable trophies

      PowerPyx and GTABase both report exactly one trophy that can be missed. Every other trophy stays earnable in free roam after the story, because there is no mission replay but the story trophies unlock automatically when their mission or chapter ends.

      Why nothing else can be missed

      Story and mission trophies unlock automatically when you finish their chapter or mission. Activities, collectibles, robberies and open-world trophies all stay available in free roam. The only moment the game takes away from you is the first meditation in Chapter 1.

      How the tracker stores your progress

      Your ticks are saved in this browser only (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere — this app makes no network request at all. Export downloads a JSON file of your earned trophies; import reads such a file back. The spoiler setting hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.
